Naming convention explained

* First part is Log profile to 709

* Second part is the conversion used, i.e. PFE LUTs or ARRI LUT package LUTs or CST

* Third part is where 'Tone Mapping' was used when using CST to convert to Cineon LOG to prep for PFE application

* Fourth part is the bit depth the image is rendered at by Resolve, here is 16bit

* Lastly is the sequence number

* File format is png
